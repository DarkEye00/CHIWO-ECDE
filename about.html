Our story

CHIWO Early Childhood Education Initiative was started in 2018 to give children in Mathare No. 10 a safe and caring place to learn while their parents work. What began as a small community effort to care for children from vulnerable families has grown into a centre serving more than 80 children and their families.

Our motto

Empowering Children, Uplifting Communities

Our mission

To give every child a loving environment where they are safe, cared for by trained teachers, given quality early learning, and supported with their basic needs.

Our values

  • Q

    Quality

    Early learning that follows the national curriculum and prepares children for primary school.

  • S

    Safety

    A secure, clean environment where every child is watched over and cared for.

  • C

    Community

    We work hand in hand with parents, because we raise our children together.

  • G

    Growth

    We nurture each child's mind, body, and confidence at their own pace.

Welcome from the head teacher

Dear parents and guardians, welcome to CHIWO. We know how much trust it takes to leave your child in someone else's care, and we don't take that lightly. Our teachers treat every child as their own, and our doors are always open to you. We look forward to partnering with you to give your child the best possible start in life.

— Head Teacher, CHIWO

Our team

Our trained ECDE teachers and caregivers teach Kenya's Competency-Based Curriculum (CBC) through songs, stories and play, in English and Kiswahili. They know every child by name.
