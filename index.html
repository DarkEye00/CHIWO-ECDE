At CHIWO, we believe it takes a community to raise a child. Parents can go to work with peace of mind, knowing their little ones are safe, well fed, and learning every day with caring, trained teachers.

Why parents choose CHIWO

  • Safe and child-friendly

    A clean, secure space where children can play, explore, and grow.

  • Affordable for all families

    Quality early learning shouldn't be out of reach, so our fees stay low and admission is free.

  • Free daily meals

    Every enrolled child gets a tea break, lunch, snacks, and fresh fruit.

  • Trained ECDE teachers

    Qualified, experienced teachers who know how young children learn.
