Programmes

From the first steps of daycare to getting ready for Grade 1, we care for and teach children at every early stage.

  • Daycare
  • Playgroup
  • PP1
  • PP2
  • Holiday Programme

Daycare

Ages 6 months – 3 years

A warm, safe place for your youngest while you work. Our professional caregivers keep children clean, fed, and engaged with play and stimulation activities that support healthy development.

Playgroup

Ages 3–4

Children learn to share, communicate, and explore through songs, stories, games, and creative play, building the social skills and confidence they need for school.

PP1

Ages 4–5

Structured early learning following the national curriculum, with a focus on early literacy, numbers, and creative activities that make learning fun.

PP2

Ages 5–6

Our final year prepares children for Grade 1, strengthening reading, writing, and number skills while building independence and a love of learning.

Holiday Programme

School holidays

Educational activities, games, arts and crafts, outdoor play and social development during school holidays.

Free feeding programme

Every enrolled child receives a tea break, lunch, snacks, and fresh fruit each day at no extra cost, because children learn best when they're well fed.
